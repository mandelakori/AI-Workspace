Review [1.0.0rc1](RELEASE-NOTES-1.0.0rc1.md), its
[Milestones A–E acceptance matrix](ACCEPTANCE.md), and the
[operator guide](OPERATOR-GUIDE.md). Resolve packaging or documentation issues
under review before an operator decision on merging or publishing.

stateful native reconstruction, and the matched live cross-plane comparison.

documented evidence. The [grant register](STATUS.md#trial-disposition) records
which historical runs are consumed, held, or unauthorized.

The [historical handoffs](NEXT-STEPS-HISTORY.md) retain the original chronology,
scope and prerequisites. The [managed Lambda setup](NEXT-STEPS-HISTORY.md#lambda-managed-profile)
and [REA integration notes](NEXT-STEPS-HISTORY.md#rea-and-rea-next) are reference
material for separate operator review.
